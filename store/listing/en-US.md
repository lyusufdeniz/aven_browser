## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Aven is a web browser designed for the TV screen first. Your remote moves a cursor across the page and OK clicks. The same Aven runs on your phone too, with a clean start screen, tabs, a reading list and a remote for your TV.

📺 EASY BROWSING ON YOUR TV
• Remote cursor: move it with the arrow keys, click with OK, press Up at the top edge to reach the address bar
• Voice search with your remote's microphone
• A start page made for the big screen: video, live and music sites one click away
• Continue watching: jump back into videos where you left off
• Open sites as desktop, mobile or Android TV

▶️ BUILT-IN AVEN PLAYER
• Play page videos full screen in the Aven player
• Playback speed, quality selection and subtitles
• Choose subtitle size, color and background
• Full-screen video, rotation and picture-in-picture on the phone

📱 FAST AND CLEAN ON YOUR PHONE
• Centred start screen, floating toolbar and tabs with site icons
• Undo a closed tab with one tap
• Reading mode and Read later: keep pages for offline reading
• Find in page, zoom, and a long-press menu for links and images
• Light, dark or system theme

📡 PHONE AND TV TOGETHER
• Turn your phone into a TV remote: drag to move the cursor, two fingers to scroll
• Type on the TV with your phone keyboard
• Send the page or video you are watching on your phone to Aven on your TV
• Devices only connect to each other on the same Wi-Fi network

🛡️ PRIVACY AND CONTROL
• Incognito tabs: pages you open there are not saved to history
• Optional ad blocking with an in-app list. It does not change your network settings or ask for extra permissions
• Site settings: manage location, camera, microphone, notification and autoplay access per site
• Connection security and certificate details
• No account, no ads, no tracking

📚 LIBRARY AND DATA
• Bookmarks, history, downloads and your reading list in one place
• Import bookmarks from Chrome, Firefox or any browser with an HTML file
• Export your bookmarks and history
• Clear history for the last 15 minutes, hour, day, week, 4 weeks or all time

⚙️ AND MORE
• Default search engine: Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Yandex
• Lite browsing mode for smoother pages on older TV boxes
• Set Aven as your default browser
• Downloads and file opening

Aven needs no account. Your history, bookmarks and settings stay on your device.
